My work moves between drawing, painting, jewelry, fashion, pattern, objects, and collected fragments. I like the moment when an idea is still a little loose — when it can become a sketch, a surface, a wearable object, or something unexpected.

I am interested in the everyday: small observations, familiar materials, color combinations, photographs, textures, clothing, flowers, rooms, and things that have been handled or carried. I often begin by collecting rather than deciding. A page fills up, a palette appears, a shape repeats, and eventually a project starts to take form.

Making by hand is important to me because it leaves room for surprise. I enjoy visible process, imperfect edges, material changes, and the little decisions that make an object feel personal.

This portfolio is a snapshot rather than a finished archive — a place for completed work, experiments, notes, and the things I am still figuring out.
